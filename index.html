About Majes Two

The story behind our passion for botanical resin craftsmanship.

Captured Botanicals. Everlasting Art.

Founded by Shrijani and Anshika, Majes Two is born out of a deep reverence for the delicate beauty found in nature. We specialize in capturing organic materials—vibrant wildflowers, leaves, bark, and premium woods—within crystal clear epoxy resin, locking their fleeting beauty in time.

Every single item is made by hand in small batches, ensuring that no two pieces are ever identical. The bubbles, wood grains, and flower alignments tell the authentic story of organic materials and painstaking manual craftsmanship.
